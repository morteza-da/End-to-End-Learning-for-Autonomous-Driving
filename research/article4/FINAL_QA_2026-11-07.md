## Critical issue found and corrected in v101
The v100 manuscript defines yaw departure as `abs(r-rk)/(abs(rk)+0.01)` in two locations, while the executable solver and stored CSV use `abs(r-rk)/max(abs(rk),0.01)`. These are **not equivalent**. The v101 manuscript corrects both locations to match the actual executable definition. Do not recompute or reinterpret published counts using the additive denominator.

The v100 Data Availability Statement pointed to a different repository and claimed a private repository. The v101 statement identifies the verified solver location at `https://github.com/morteza-da/End-to-End-Learning-for-Autonomous-Driving/tree/main/research/article4` and explicitly states that the full reproducibility bundle is not yet verified as deposited.
